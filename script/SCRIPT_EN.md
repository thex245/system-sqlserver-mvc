# DO YOU STILL LOVE ME?

### Analog horror short — English localization

**TAPE B.C.**

`V:` robotic narrator (monotone synthetic voice) · `H:` human voice · `[ ]` on-screen text

---

The screen stays black.

```
[NOTICE — RESTRICTED ACCESS]

CLASSIFIED DOCUMENT
PROPERTY OF THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT
ACCESS LEVEL: B.C.

Reproduction, copying or distribution of this material without
authorization constitutes a violation of the National Security Act.
```

VHS tape noise rises. The image fades in slowly.

```
[FILE: B.C. — HISTORY AND BEHAVIOR]
```

**V:** This material was produced for the instruction of personnel involved in the identification, containment, and interaction with organisms classified as PASIÓN.

---

## SLIDE 01 — DEFINITION

An extremely grainy photograph. A human figure stands deep in a forest. Its face has been completely censored.

**V:** Pasión is the designation used for organisms of unknown origin, capable of exhibiting imitative, adaptive, and affective behavior.

*Pause.*

**V:** Although they present distinct physical characteristics, all observed variants have demonstrated one recurring need.

The screen flickers.

**V:** BOND.

---

## SLIDE 02 — EARLIEST RECORDS

Photographs of cave paintings, one after another. Animals. Hunters. People around fires. Among them, a humanoid figure appears again and again — always next to a person.

**V:** Archaeological evidence suggests that Pasión organisms may have existed on Earth for approximately forty thousand years.

One photograph is enlarged: a figure embracing another.

**V:** Similar depictions have been identified across different regions and historical periods.

Another image: a human figure seems to kneel before something.

**V:** The records share one common characteristic.

The audio distorts slightly.

**V:** The creature is never alone.

---

## SLIDE 03 — HISTORICAL RECORDS

Old photographs. Medieval paintings. Family portraits. 19th-century photographs. War photographs. In each one, certain faces are censored.

**V:** Identification of possible Pasión individuals remains inconclusive.

The censored faces appear faster and faster.

**V:** Some individuals remained close to human communities for decades.

One photograph stays on screen: a family posing. Everyone looks at the camera. One face is censored.

**V:** In certain cases, their presence was only identified after the disappearance, or death, of associated individuals.

---

## SLIDE 04 — FIRST DOCUMENTED CONTACT

Low-quality footage. A containment room. A humanoid creature sits on the floor, completely still.

**V:** First specimen captured alive.

```
P-04
```

An employee enters the room. The creature slowly raises its head.

**V:** During initial testing, the specimen showed minimal resistance to containment.

The employee approaches. The creature extends its hand. The employee hesitates.

**V:** However, after approximately three minutes of eye contact...

The creature begins to smile. The image freezes.

**V:** ...its behavior changed.

---

## SLIDE 05 — BEHAVIOR

```
OBSERVED BEHAVIORS:
- Vocal mimicry.
- Facial mimicry.
- Morphological alteration.
- Prolonged observation.
- Pursuit.
- Attachment.
- Dependency.
- Territorial behavior.
- Aggressive reaction to rejection.
```

The word "REJECTION" remains on screen. The audio drops slightly.

**V:** Personnel must understand that the ability to imitate human emotions does not mean the organism understands them.

*Pause.*

**V:** Pasión does not love.

The screen glitches.

**V:** Pasión learns to love.

---

## SLIDE 06 — INTERACTION PROTOCOL

```
PROTOCOL 04-B
```

**V:** Authorized personnel must follow the procedures below.

Each rule appears slowly.

```
01. Do not remain alone with a specimen.
02. Do not establish physical contact without authorization.
03. Do not provide personal information.
04. Do not mention family members, partners, or emotionally significant persons.
05. Do not answer questions related to feelings.
06. Do not accept gifts offered by the specimen.
07. Do not promise to return.
08. Do not say "I love you."
```

The screen goes black. Silence.

**V:** Under no circumstances.

---

## SLIDE 07 — INCIDENT 12

A photograph of a laboratory corridor.

**V:** In 1997, an employee violated Protocol 04-B.

Security camera footage: a man sits in front of a cell. On the other side of the glass, the creature watches.

**V:** The employee remained with the specimen for four hours.

The creature places its hand on the glass. The man does the same.

**V:** During the subsequent interview, the employee stated...

The recording distorts. A human voice, very low:

**H:** She just wanted to be loved.

*Silence.*

**V:** The employee was removed from the facility.

*Pause.*

**V:** The specimen was removed from the facility.

---

## SLIDE 08 — OBSERVATION

A photograph: an empty corridor. At the end of it, a silhouette.

**V:** The specimen was never located.

The photograph remains. A small noise.

**V:** Neither was the employee.

---

## SLIDE 09 — CLASSIFICATION

```
PASIÓN
ORIGIN: UNKNOWN
OBJECTIVE: UNKNOWN
```

After a few seconds, a fourth line appears:

```
BEHAVIORAL PATTERN: LOVE
```

The text flickers.

```
AUTOMATIC CORRECTION
BEHAVIORAL PATTERN: NEED FOR LOVE
```

---

## SLIDE 10 — FINAL INSTRUCTION

The robotic voice begins to fail.

**V:** If a Pasión individual assumes the appearance of someone you know...

*Noise.*

**V:** ...do not try to find out if it is really them.

*Louder noise.*

**V:** Do not speak to it.

*Pause.*

**V:** Do not touch it.

*Pause.*

**V:** Do not show fear.

The image begins to distort.

**V:** And, above all...

*Silence.* The voice changes. For a fraction of a second it stops being robotic. It sounds human.

**H (the narrator's voice, unprocessed):** ...do not tell her you still love her.

The screen goes black.

```
[END OF FILE B.C.]
```

For a few seconds, there is no image. Then a final message:

```
FILE CORRUPTED

REASON:
"THE DOCUMENT SHOULD NOT HAVE BEEN OPENED."
```

End of tape.
