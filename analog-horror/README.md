# Season 1 — *Do You Still Love Me?* · Tape 1 — "I Do"

A procedurally produced analog-horror short (English localization), built from the script
*"Temporada 1 — Você ainda me ama? / Fita 1 — 'Eu aceito'"*.

Everything is generated by the scripts in this folder: the restaurant set, the 17 performers and
their animation, the dialogue, the sound design, the CCTV camera work and the VHS degradation.

## Creative direction

* **One camera, CAM 04**: a corner PTZ dome that zooms and auto-tracks *by itself*. Nobody operates it.
  The zooms are how the film shows faces, hands and the ring.
* **The woman never blinks**, until the last shot. Her smile is mouth-only ("perfectly normal, maybe
  too normal"). The only unnaturally smooth motion in the film is her slow head turn to the lens.
* **Nobody reacts.** The murmur bed loops a little too perfectly. During the scream the room sounds
  exactly the same, and the waiter walks past without looking.
* A public-domain 1924 recording of *"Let Me Call You Sweetheart"* plays from a ceiling speaker. In the
  empty restaurant it keeps playing: slowed, warped and stuck on the chorus.
* **The clock lies.** The picture returns at 21:51:47, *earlier* than the scream. The wall clock's
  second hand follows the CCTV clock and stops in the empty restaurant.
* **After the freeze** her face is physically pulled toward the lens (3D Warp deformer), then
  degraded in 2D (bulge, radial smear, block displacement, chroma split).

## Pipeline

| Stage | Script | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dialogue | `audio/lines.py`, `audio/tts_generate.py`, `audio/score_takes.py` | Chatterbox TTS, voice cloned from public-domain LibriVox readers; several takes per line, ranked by Whisper intelligibility + SQUIM PESQ |
| Lip sync | `audio/lipsync.py` | Whisper word timings + CMUdict phonemes → 15 Oculus visemes with co-articulation; loudness drives the jaw (JALI-style jaw/lip split) |
| Set | `blender/build_set.py`, `blender/setlib.py`, `blender/layout.py` | Procedural room + Poly Haven models/textures, cloth-simulated tablecloths |
| Cast | `blender/cast.py`, `blender/bake_cast.py`, `blender/anim.py`, `blender/rbx.py` | Microsoft Rocketbox avatars with ARKit/FACS/viseme blendshapes; Rocketbox mocap retargeted in world space |
| Performance | `blender/assemble*.py` | NLA clip blending, gaze/saccades/eye-head coordination, speech accents, hand IK (ring removal, table slam), props |
| QA | `blender/qa_contacts.py` | Measures hand/forearm/knee/back interpenetration with tables and chairs over the whole film |
| Render | `blender/render_film.py` | Resumable Eevee render, 30 fps CCTV, separate freeze pass |
| Post | `post/compose.py`, `post/fx.py`, `post/osd.py` | CCTV grading, chroma smear, line jitter, head switching, OSD, glitch events |
| Mix | `audio/mix.py`, `audio/sfx.py` | Ceiling-mic + VHS chain, procedural Foley, freeze distortion |

All timings live in `timeline.py`.
